"NVIDIA always pops on earnings." I hear this constantly.

Checked it properly: all 71 of its earnings reports since 2006, measured against the market and its own sector peers. It beats them on 44% to 55% of occasions depending on the window. That's not "always." That's close to a coin flip.

Fair pushback: maybe that's just diluted by years nobody was watching. So I split it at the obvious line — before 2023, and the AI earnings supercycle since. If the story's real, the recent stretch should look better, at least against its own peers. It doesn't. NVIDIA's one-month return around earnings, relative to AMD and the semiconductor sector, is actually worse in the AI era than before it.

Didn't stop there. Checked whether AI stocks move together more now than before ChatGPT — less, not more, and NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, and Apple don't even predict each other's bad days anymore. Checked whether there's anything in the minute-by-minute data worth chasing, using the same two methods that already came up empty on crypto. Same empty result, equities this time.

Three separate checks, three different timescales. None of them found what the story says should be there.

The stock prices went up regardless — that part's real. I just can't find the trading mechanism everyone assumes comes with it.
