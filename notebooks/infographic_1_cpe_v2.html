Most quantitative finance demands a forecast. CPE asks a different question — and gets a more honest answer.
CPE(Y, X, τ) = P( ΔYfuture > rqY | ΔXpast > rqX )
The probability Y makes a large future move, given X made a large past move — computed as a direct historical count, no assumptions.
