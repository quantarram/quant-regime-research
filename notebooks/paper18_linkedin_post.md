A Singapore Category A COE just cleared at S$132,450. The highest in the 24 years of results I have.

Everyone has a theory about why. I tested them: rules written down first, graded only on years they had never seen.

The theory that fits is almost boring. A COE lasts ten years. Cars sold in a boom come due ten years later, the quota is set from cars leaving the road, and the premium follows supply with a lag of 18 to 24 months. The market is its own echo, with a full swing about every 11.6 years.

Only one trading rule survived grading. After a quota cut, the premium is in its biggest jumps 49% of the time a year later, against 24% normally. The asterisk is in the paper: it held until 2021 and I cannot see it since 2022.

Property did worse. 1,622 condition-years of out-of-sample tests back to 1975. Momentum, rents, rates and vacancy all looked brilliant before 2017 and fell apart after. Survivors: none.

So the dashboard's verdict is WAIT & WATCH, and it logs its own forecasts in public. First scores arrive in early 2027.
