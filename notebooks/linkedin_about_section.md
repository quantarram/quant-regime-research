Beyond CPE:
I took my doctoral multifractal cascade work into finance and found predictability in pockets, not the atmosphere's steady fade. It runs live on 22 instruments: real skill for half, no tradeable alpha. It also yields an AI/ML training-window rule. Five attempts to beat the ceiling (architecture, depth, data, loss, uncertainty) failed; a sixth, at the decision layer, hit the same wall. Using the limit to pick which instruments a momentum strategy trades did beat the unfiltered version across ten tests.

Failures get equal billing:
Paper 7's Sugar Reversal was a geographic artefact. The hurricane study backed one of two hypotheses. Three AI-stock assumptions came back empty. Everyday weather carries no price signal (Paper 17), so rare extremes stay open. And no Singapore housing timing signal survives out of sample (Paper 18).
