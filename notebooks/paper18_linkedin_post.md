A Singapore Category A COE just cleared at S$132,450. The highest in 24 years of results.

Everyone has a theory. I graded them on years they had never seen.

The one that fits is almost insulting in its simplicity. A COE lasts ten years. Boom-year cars come due a decade later, the quota is set by cars leaving the road, and the premium follows 18 to 24 months behind. Singapore's most expensive piece of paper is just its own echo, swinging about every 11.6 years.

One trading rule survived. After a quota cut, the premium makes its biggest jumps 49% of the time a year later, against 24% normally. The asterisk: I cannot find it since 2022. Even echoes change their mind.

Property was a bloodbath. 1,622 condition-years of tests back to 1975. Momentum, rents, rates and vacancy looked like geniuses before 2017 and idiots after. Survivors: zero.

So the dashboard says WAIT & WATCH, which is finance for "nobody knows, including me". It logs its own forecasts in public. First report card: early 2027.
